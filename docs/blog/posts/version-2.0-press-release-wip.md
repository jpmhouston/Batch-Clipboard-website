# Version 2.0 Press Release (work in progress)

One more fix and beta 3 might be ready (*), and if no show-stoppers are found then 2.0 final will follow.

*(\* also, considering one other larger change, someone talk me out of it)*

Working on a press release. Feedback anyone?

> Batch Clipboard is already less than a clipboard manager. Less complication, less new UI you need to learn, less for you to manage. but gives your your Mac clipboard a "serial paste" or "queue" super-power.
> 
> Now version 2.0 brings you more “less”! Less performance hit to your system, less duplication of features with macOS 26, less complexity in the menu behind its menu bar icon, and if you want to, hide that icon when you don’t need it for less space taken up in your menu bar.
> 
> But also more, “Repeat Last Batch” to paste the whole last set of things again, and turn the clipboard history on after all if you want (if you’re stuck on Sequoia or older maybe, the app works on systems as far back as 10.15 Catalina).
> 
> And even more “more” if you support its development with a one-time  in-app purchase: save the last batch to recall anytime you like. In fact save as many as you like to recall from the menu or give each a custom keyboard shortcut!
> 
> More than before and less than ever. So to celebrate “more” and “less”, that in-app purchase for more is now priced less for the next 2 weeks: US$1.99 so (more or less) half price!
